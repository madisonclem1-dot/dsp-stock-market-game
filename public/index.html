GAME GUIDE

How to play

Learn real investing concepts with fictional market prices. No experience required.

1 · Join

Choose a unique name before the host starts. Up to 30 players can join.

2 · Invest

Start with $10,000. Buy whole units of 60 investments. Cash can stay uninvested. CD purchases lock for two news events.

3 · Learn & predict

When breaking news appears, trading pauses. Read the lesson and answer a question worth 100 knowledge XP.

4 · See the reaction

The host reveals the market response. Review what happened, then trade again with your new knowledge.

5 · Compete

Wealth rank uses portfolio value. Knowledge rank uses XP. Overall rank combines 70% wealth ranking and 30% knowledge ranking.

6 · Reflect

Compare your results to a passive broad-market index investor. Remember: higher returns do not always mean better risk management.

All prices, headlines, outcomes, and interest rates are simulated. This game is for education, not financial advice.

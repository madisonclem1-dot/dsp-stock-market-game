How to play

No experience needed. Learn by trading.

1. Join

Enter your name before the host starts. Maximum 30 players.

2. Invest

Use your fictional $10,000 to buy whole shares. You can hold cash too.

3. React

Each news round changes prices. Decide whether to buy, sell, or hold.

4. Win

Highest total portfolio value at the end wins, including remaining cash.

Stocks, prices, headlines, and returns are fictional. This is an educational game, not investment advice.
